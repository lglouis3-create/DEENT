## Purpose of the questions (Louis, 2026-11-12; governs every question)
"the purposes of reviewing the questions/ concepts from class of any material given is to serve as a basis/ reference that builds/ to raises the difficulty. the reason for a raising the difficulty is to provide the students reassurance in knowing vs memorizing the material. I mainly want us to use all the material (especially the transcript and slides/ required readings) to guide the questions on how to ensure students know the material. Next for it to predict and select the most likely questions/ topics/ concepts the professor will test on." Research in other repos and credible sources is allowed.
- Polls, recalled exam questions and in-class questions are the floor, not the target: each is kept verbatim once, then built on (new patient, new numbers, the reverse direction, the next step, two facts combined) so that a right answer shows the student knows the concept rather than the remembered item. Matching a professor's surface habits is not a goal.
- Every question is written from the slides, the transcript and the required readings together; the transcript decides what the professor stressed.
- Prediction: rank topics and concepts by the evidence that the professor will test them (her objectives slides, polls, "hint"/"know this"/"this will be on the exam" statements, repetition and time spent in the transcript, cases worked in class) and put the most likely first (stars, Topics order, practice papers).
- Item-writing reference: NBME Item-Writing Guide (Constructing Written Test Questions), e.g. the "cover-the-options" rule: a well-focused stem can be answered before the options are seen. https://info.nbme.org/rs/552-QHC-046/images/NBME_Item-Writing-Guide.pdf
