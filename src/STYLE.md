### Polls and in-class questions, 10/29 (Skin-Pathophys-Students-2026.pdf slide 38)

- PollEv 1: "What is one of the major pathologies of atopic dermatitis?"  options: Skin barrier dysfuntion / Karatinocyte hyperproliferation / Excess sebum production  answer: Skin barrier dysfunction (final room result 95% / 4% / 1%).
- PollEv 2: "What is one of the major pathologies of psoriasis?"  same options  answer: Keratinocyte hyperproliferation.
- PollEv 3: "What is one of the major pathologies of acne?"  same options  answer: Excess sebum production. She added: keratinocyte hyperproliferation "is not one of the components of acne. ... This is a feature of psoriasis" (T 573–577).
- Slide 15 (T 226–231): "So I told you guys that many of our atopic dermatitis patients don't have that FLG gene variant, but they've got atopic dermatitis. What would that make you think?"  answer: other variants in FLG not yet identified, or another protein made or not made.
- Slide 19 (T 295–297): "What's going on in the skin for people with atopic dermatitis? It's not hyperproliferation and incomplete differentiation. What is it?"  answer: a structural problem from too little profilaggrin and filaggrins.
- PCol slide 12 (T 852–853): "You can open up your patient to the possibility of infections. Why does that happen?"  answer: these drugs are immunosuppressive.
- PCol slides 8–12 (T 873–875): "what's the purpose of using topical corticosteroids for treating atopic dermatitis and psoriasis?"  answer: to suppress the activity of the immune system.
